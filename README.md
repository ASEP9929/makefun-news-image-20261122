# The World in One Day — 22 November 2026

A make.fun bounty entry for [news-image-20261122](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261122): one image summing up the world news of Sunday, 22 November 2026.

The image is a front page, drawn fresh in this session: a masthead naming the day, a lead story on the Gulf escalation, and five more panels covering the Baltic, the UN, the climate talks, Mexico, and Peru.

## The six stories

1. **Gulf war flares again** — Iran hits four US bases in Bahrain on Saturday night as tankers burn in the Strait of Hormuz; the IRGC says it shot down two US drones. (euronews / ANI / Al Jazeera)
2. **Drones set Russian ship ablaze** — the cargo ship Sparta II is hit by drones in the Baltic, catches fire and drifts into Finnish waters; Finland tightens security and says Ukraine was likely behind the attack. (Reuters)
3. **France takes Myanmar to the UN** — Paris seeks a Security Council meeting after Myanmar rejects the World Court's ruling that its military committed genocide against the Rohingya. (Reuters)
4. **Climate talks deadlock** — the COP31 talks stall as the fight over fossil fuels blocks a deal. (BBC)
5. **El Chapo Isidro captured** — Mexican forces arrest Fausto Isidro Meza Flores, one of the FBI's ten most wanted fugitives; 11 are killed as violence erupts in Sinaloa. (Reuters)
6. **Peru shaken** — a magnitude 6.9 earthquake hits the Andes in central Peru; tremors are felt in Lima, with no casualties reported. (USGS / Reuters)

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies the shipped `entry.png` byte-identically to the output directory (`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`, default `/output`). The image is 1024×1024 PNG, 91,603 bytes, and two runs produce byte-identical output.
